# Community cosmetics: licences and permissions

Audit of 29 September 2026 against catalogue `collection-v5`: 159 shipped items from 72 sources.
It is generated from `assets/client/cosmetics/credits/`, `CREDITS.json` and the catalogue; those files stay the source of truth.
This is a record of what the credit files say, not legal advice.

## What to do

1. **Group D (14 items, two packs by Foxxy.)**: `gamebanana.com/mods/169749` (OpenSpades: Winter Warfare, 11 items) and `gamebanana.com/mods/170869` (BF4 Assault Class, 3 items). Both also carry a CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 notice, which on its own would allow unmodified sharing; the author's checklist says no, and this audit follows the stricter of the two. Disable them in the next catalogue unless the author gives written permission.
2. **Group C (31 items)**: 28 came from local archives with no author, source or licence recorded (AWP, Axe, Pump-action shotgun, AK-47 Paratrooper, AK47, A stick, Pala 2.0, the Sans & Papyrus set, Deuce Remastered, the SAF character set). Find where each came from and ask, or disable them. The other 3 (`mods/170191`, `mods/170516`, `mods/170950`) state that redistribution and changes need the author's permission and credit.
3. **Group B (112 items)**: keep the credit, source link and licence link with every item (the site's `/credits` page and the credit files do this). For each item whose shipped files differ from the author's archive, either ask the author or ship the original file. Format conversion alone is not a change of the work; retuning, refitting and repainting are.
4. **Group A (2 items)**: `community-sep-170563-1-v5` (GPL-3.0-or-later, part of OpenSpades) and `community-sep-315714-1-v5` (free to use with credit).

Disabling an item keeps it as an owned historical item with the base appearance (see [COMMUNITY_COSMETICS.md](COMMUNITY_COSMETICS.md)); it does not rewrite receipts.
